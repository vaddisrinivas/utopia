# Code of Conduct

Utopia is meant to stay useful, calm, and reviewable. Everyone who participates in the project is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and truthful.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Keep feedback focused on behavior, code, docs, and decisions.
- Protect privacy and avoid exposing personal, receipt, health, account, or token data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, intimidation, or sexualized language.
- Public shaming, doxxing, or repeated disruption.
- Deliberate misinformation or bad-faith argument.
- Sharing secrets or private user data.

## Reporting problems

- For normal conduct concerns, contact the project maintainer privately if you can.
- For security or data exposure concerns, use the private reporting path in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, remove content, close issues, mute participation, or block access when behavior harms the project or its users.
